\section{Grouping Signals Favor Different Outcomes}
\label{app:ggp}
\setcounter{table}{0}
\renewcommand{\thetable}{\Alph{section}.\arabic{table}}

The direction-only control separates directional convergence ranking from
representative-based membership. Table~\ref{tab:app:ggp} reports all three
grouping rules on the same 160 runs and 5440 checkpoints from
Section~\ref{sec:exp:ggp}. It retains the same 25-solution quota, run-level
aggregation, and Holm correction across 32 comparisons.

Direction-only grouping achieves higher current convergence precision than
PAQC in all eight configurations, with overall means of 95.97\% and 73.68\%.
PAQC achieves higher overall final-retention precision, 53.58\% versus 50.07\%,
and higher mean retention in seven configurations. Holm-adjusted tests favor
PAQC in three configurations and detect no difference in the other five.
DTLZ4 provides the largest retention gains over direction: 13.01 and
9.89 percentage points at $M=10$ and 20, with all 20 paired runs favoring
PAQC at each objective count. Direction has a 0.48-point mean retention
advantage on DTLZ7 at $M=10$, without a detected difference after correction.

These results associate representative membership with greater subsequent
retention while directional ranking supplies stronger low-$g$ discrimination.
The recorded checkpoints compare group composition along PACDIS trajectories;
comparing final IGD$^+$ after replacing PAQC with direction-only grouping
requires an online control.
