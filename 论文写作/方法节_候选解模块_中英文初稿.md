## 一、中文正文

## 4 基于尺度自适应批量控制的双路候选选择

关系模型可以在大量未评价解之间建立相对偏好，但其输出分数的分布会随问题、搜索阶段和当前训练集变化。如果直接使用固定绝对阈值，分数尺度的变化将被传递到真实评价批量：同一阈值可能在一个问题上选择大量候选，而在另一个问题上长期触发小批量回退。本文采用**分位筛选与有界批量相结合的双路候选选择机制**，将“候选排序”和“每轮投入多少真实评价”分开处理。关系模型负责在代理搜索中形成候选优先级，分位规则将不同尺度的分数转换为相对排名，固定区间 $[n_{\min},n_{\max}]$ 则约束每轮真实评价量。两个候选路径分别引入预测模糊度与决策空间距离、以及前沿形状感知的 SDE 指标偏好，并通过随机流在统一候选池上交替工作。

该设计直接针对固定阈值的尺度敏感性。设关系网络的三类输出均为概率单纯形上的向量，则本文使用的关系净证据分数严格落在 $[-4,4]$。因此，REMO 式规则中的固定阈值 $3.9$ 位于理论上限的 $97.5\%$。在由 4 个变体、5 个问题和 20 次独立运行组成的 400-run 控制审计中，不执行该阈值、只记录诊断量的控制臂显示：同一阈值对应的合格候选比例跨问题相差 $5.1$ 倍；若按该阈值分配批量，相同的 200 次追加真实评价将产生 14.8--46.2 个代理辅助搜索轮次，相差 $3.1$ 倍。本文据此以分位数确定候选集合，并显式限制每轮评价数量，使评价投入不再由绝对分数尺度直接决定。

### 4.1 面向粗质量组的关系净证据

设第 3 节得到的当前种群正组与非正组分别为

$$
\mathcal{C}_1=\{\mathbf{x}_i\mid c_i=1\},
\qquad
\mathcal{C}_2=\{\mathbf{x}_i\mid c_i\neq 1\}.
$$

关系网络对一个有序解对 $(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b)$ 输出

$$
\boldsymbol{\pi}(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b)
=
\left[
\pi_{+1}(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b),
\pi_{0}(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b),
\pi_{-1}(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b)
\right],
$$

其中三个分量分别对应“前者优于后者”“二者属于同一粗质量组”和“后者优于前者”。对于任一未评价候选解 $\mathbf{x}$，算法同时构造四组有序比较：

$$
(\mathcal{C}_1,\mathbf{x}),\quad
(\mathbf{x},\mathcal{C}_1),\quad
(\mathcal{C}_2,\mathbf{x}),\quad
(\mathbf{x},\mathcal{C}_2).
$$

记这四组预测概率的组内均值分别为 $\mathbf{a}(\mathbf{x})$、$\mathbf{b}(\mathbf{x})$、$\mathbf{c}(\mathbf{x})$ 和 $\mathbf{d}(\mathbf{x})$。候选解的关系净证据定义为

$$
r(\mathbf{x})
=2\left[
c_{-1}(\mathbf{x})+d_{+1}(\mathbf{x})
-a_{+1}(\mathbf{x})-b_{-1}(\mathbf{x})
\right].
\tag{10}
$$

式 (10) 汇总了候选相对正组和非正组的双向关系证据。较大的 $r(\mathbf{x})$ 表示候选更经常被预测为不劣于正组，或优于非正组。由于每个预测向量的分量之和为 1，式 (10) 满足

$$
-4\le r(\mathbf{x})\le 4.
\tag{11}
$$

在探索路径中，组内均值进一步使用每个关系预测的最大类别概率作为权重，使类别分布更集中的关系对在净证据中获得更高权重。该加权只改变同一组内关系证据的聚合方式，不改变三类关系的语义。

### 4.2 代理辅助候选池构造

候选池通过关系模型引导的内部进化搜索生成。算法首先以当前种群和参考解为父代执行遗传操作，得到初始候选集合 $\mathcal{Q}^{(0)}$。在第 $\ell$ 个内部搜索轮次，关系网络计算 $r(\mathbf{x})$，并从 $\mathcal{Q}^{(\ell)}$ 中保留排名最高的

$$
n_{\mathrm{parent}}
=\min\left(|\mathcal{R}|,|\mathcal{Q}^{(\ell)}|\right)
$$

个候选，与参考解集合 $\mathcal{R}$ 共同产生下一轮候选。所有内部轮次产生的候选均并入

$$
\mathcal{A}
=\operatorname{unique}
\left(
\bigcup_{\ell=0}^{L}\mathcal{Q}^{(\ell)}
\right),
\tag{12}
$$

其中 $L$ 由累计候选上限 $g_{\max}$ 决定。集合 $\mathcal{A}$ 是两个最终选择路径共享的候选空间，使不同路径只改变评价偏好，而不改变可选候选集合。

### 4.3 预测模糊度与距离协同的探索路径

探索路径同时利用关系净证据、预测模糊度和批内距离。对候选解 $\mathbf{x}$，令 $\mathcal{O}(\mathbf{x})$ 表示其与 $\mathcal{C}_1$、$\mathcal{C}_2$ 形成的全部有序关系对，定义平均预测模糊度为

$$
u(\mathbf{x})
=1-
\frac{1}{|\mathcal{O}(\mathbf{x})|}
\sum_{(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b)\in\mathcal{O}(\mathbf{x})}
\max_{y\in\{-1,0,+1\}}
\pi_y(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b).
\tag{13}
$$

较大的 $u(\mathbf{x})$ 表示关系网络在该候选附近产生更平缓的类别分布。令 $\widetilde r(\mathbf{x})$ 和 $\widetilde u(\mathbf{x})$ 分别表示候选池内的 min--max 归一化结果，探索得分写为

$$
a^{\mathrm{E}}(\mathbf{x})
=\widetilde r(\mathbf{x})
+\lambda_t\widetilde u(\mathbf{x}),
\tag{14}
$$

其中

$$
\lambda_t
=\lambda_0
\left(1-\frac{FE}{B}\right)
\max\left(0,1-\frac{p_{\mathrm{err}}}{0.45}\right).
\tag{15}
$$

$B$ 为最大真实评价预算，$p_{\mathrm{err}}$ 为关系对留出集上的分类错误率。式 (15) 在搜索前段保留更强的模糊度采样，并在关系模型错误率升高时自动收缩该项，使探索强度同时受预算进度和当前关系模型状态控制。

算法使用 $a^{\mathrm{E}}$ 的 $q_{\mathrm{keep}}$ 分位数构造预选集合：

$$
\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{E}}
=\left\{
\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{A}
\mid
a^{\mathrm{E}}(\mathbf{x})
\ge Q_{q_{\mathrm{keep}}}
\left(a^{\mathrm{E}}(\mathcal{A})\right)
\right\}.
\tag{16}
$$

本文取 $q_{\mathrm{keep}}=0.80$。当预选候选不足 $n_{\min}$ 时，直接补入探索得分最高的候选。随后采用质量--距离贪心规则组装评价批次。第一个候选取 $a^{\mathrm{E}}$ 最大者；其余候选依次最大化

$$
A(\mathbf{x}\mid\mathcal{S})
=0.75\,\widehat a^{\mathrm{E}}(\mathbf{x})
+0.25\,
\widehat d(\mathbf{x},\mathcal{S}),
\tag{17}
$$

其中

$$
d(\mathbf{x},\mathcal{S})
=\min_{\mathbf{z}\in\mathcal{S}}
\|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{z}\|_2,
$$

$\widehat a^{\mathrm{E}}$ 与 $\widehat d$ 是当前剩余候选上的归一化值。式 (17) 以关系质量为主，同时避免一个评价批次集中在决策空间的同一局部区域。

### 4.4 前沿形状感知的指标路径

指标路径从当前已评价种群中构造一个独立于关系网络输出的重排序信号。首先使用第一非支配前沿估计广义 $L_p$ 形状参数，并据此计算 SDE 适应度 $\phi_i$ `[PIEA]`。对于归一化目标向量 $\bar{\mathbf{f}}_i$，SDE 距离以逐目标移位后的最近邻距离为基础：

$$
\delta_i
=\min_{j\ne i}
\left\|
\bar{\mathbf{f}}_i-
\max\left(\bar{\mathbf{f}}_i,\bar{\mathbf{f}}_j\right)
\right\|_2.
\tag{18}
$$

当 $\delta_i$ 接近零时，算法使用到当前理想点的 $L_p$ 距离补充区分度。归一化后的 $\phi_i$ 用于训练 RBF-SVR：

$$
\widehat\phi(\mathbf{x})
=\operatorname{SVR}(\mathbf{x};\mathcal{P},\boldsymbol{\phi}).
\tag{19}
$$

指标路径先按关系净证据保留候选池中排名最高的 $30\%$，且粗筛集合不少于 20 个候选；随后使用 $\widehat\phi$ 重排序，并保留不低于其 $70\%$ 分位数的候选。该顺序使关系模型负责排除明显低优先级区域，SDE 代理则在缩小后的候选集合中表达前沿形状与局部密度偏好。

### 4.5 双路随机选择与有界真实评价批次

设 $m_t\in\{\mathrm{E},\mathrm{I}\}$ 表示第 $t$ 次外层迭代采用的候选路径。算法从与运行编号绑定的独立随机流采样 $\xi_t\sim U[0,1)$，并按

$$
m_t=
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{I}, & \text{若指标模型可用且 }\xi_t<p_{\mathrm{mix}},\\
\mathrm{E}, & \text{其他情况}
\end{cases}
\tag{20}
$$

确定当前路径。本文取 $p_{\mathrm{mix}}=0.5$，使两种候选偏好获得相同的先验使用机会；当指标模型不可用时，算法直接使用探索路径。

无论采用哪一路径，最终真实评价数量均限制为

$$
n_t
=\min\left[
n_{\max},
\max\left(n_{\min},|\mathcal{H}^{m_t}|\right)
\right],
\tag{21}
$$

并进一步按剩余真实评价预算截断。本文设置 $n_{\min}=4$、$n_{\max}=6$。因此，关系分数和指标分数只决定批次内部的候选优先级，不再直接决定一次外层迭代消耗多少真实评价。若最终候选集合为空，则以当前种群和参考解执行一次遗传操作，并返回不超过 $n_{\min}$ 个候选，从而保持搜索循环连续。

候选级正式诊断为这一设计提供了直接支撑。在 4 个 20 目标问题、每个问题 10 次独立运行的共享宿主比较中，相对于在同一累积候选池上直接选取关系分数 top-6 的参照，双路模块将后半程候选存活率从 $0.706$ 提高到 $0.770$（配对 Wilcoxon 符号秩检验，$p=0.0085$），并将相对贪心 oracle 的后半程增益比从 $0.0861$ 提高到 $0.1048$（$p=0.0250$）。前者记录新评价解被环境选择接纳的比例，后者度量所选批次相对于同一候选池可达收益的比例；二者共同表明，有界双路选择改变了候选批次的实际质量结构，而非只改变模式名称。

### 4.6 算法流程与计算复杂度

算法 2 总结候选解模块。设当前种群规模为 $N$，去重候选池规模为 $N_c$，决策变量数为 $D$，目标数为 $M$。对所有候选构造双向关系对需要

$$
2N_c(|\mathcal{C}_1|+|\mathcal{C}_2|)=2N_cN
$$

个网络输入，因此关系打分的数据构造与存储复杂度为 $O(N_cND)$。SDE 适应度计算需要两两目标距离，复杂度为 $O(N^2M)$。指标路径还包含一次基于 $N$ 个已评价样本的 SVR 拟合，以及对粗筛候选的预测。质量--距离贪心批选择最多选择 $n_{\max}$ 个候选，其距离计算复杂度为 $O(n_{\max}N_cD)$。候选模块不改变真实评价总预算；其新增计算发生在代理预测、SDE 计算和候选重排序阶段。

```text
Algorithm 2  Scale-adaptive dual-route candidate selection
Input: population P, reference set R, relation model fθ,
       error estimate p_err, FE budget B, g_max,
       q_keep, λ0, p_mix, n_min, n_max
Output: candidate batch S

1:  Generate Q(0) from P ∪ R and set A ← Q(0)
2:  while the number of generated candidates is below g_max do
3:      Compute relation evidence r(x) for x ∈ Q(ℓ)
4:      Retain the top min(|R|, |Q(ℓ)|) candidates as parents
5:      Generate Q(ℓ+1) and update A ← unique(A ∪ Q(ℓ+1))
6:  end while
7:  Fit the SDE-SVR model from the evaluated population when available
8:  Draw ξ from the run-specific random stream
9:  if the indicator model is available and ξ < p_mix then
10:     Relation-prefilter A and rank the retained candidates by SDE-SVR
11: else
12:     Compute ambiguity-aware score aE and its q_keep quantile set
13:     Assemble the batch by the quality–distance greedy rule
14: end if
15: Bound the batch size to [n_min,n_max] and truncate it by the remaining FE
16: Return S
```

## II. English Draft

## 4 Scale-adaptive dual-route candidate selection

The relation model establishes pairwise preferences among a large number of unevaluated solutions, whereas the distribution of its output score varies with the problem, search stage, and current training set. An absolute cutoff transfers this scale variation directly to the number of expensive evaluations allocated in each iteration. To decouple candidate ranking from evaluation-batch size, we develop a **scale-adaptive dual-route candidate selection mechanism**. The relation model determines candidate priority during surrogate-assisted search, quantile rules convert scores of different scales into within-pool ranks, and an explicit interval $[n_{\min},n_{\max}]$ controls the number of expensive evaluations. Two selection routes then operate on the same candidate pool: an ambiguity-and-distance-aware exploration route and a front-shape-aware indicator route.

This design is motivated by the score geometry itself. Because each relation-network output lies on a three-class probability simplex, the net relation-evidence score used in this work is bounded by $[-4,4]$. The fixed cutoff of 3.9 used by the REMO-style rule therefore lies at $97.5\%$ of the theoretical upper bound. In a 400-run controlled audit comprising four variants, five problems, and 20 independent runs, the diagnostic arm that recorded but did not apply the cutoff showed a $5.1$-fold cross-problem variation in the passing proportion. Applying the cutoff to batch allocation would make the same 200 additional expensive evaluations produce 14.8--46.2 surrogate-assisted search iterations, a $3.1$-fold difference. Our selector uses quantile filtering and an explicitly bounded batch so that absolute score calibration no longer directly determines evaluation expenditure.

### 4.1 Group-relative relation evidence

Let the positive and non-positive groups produced in Section 3 be

$$
\mathcal{C}_1=\{\mathbf{x}_i\mid c_i=1\},
\qquad
\mathcal{C}_2=\{\mathbf{x}_i\mid c_i\neq1\}.
$$

For an ordered pair $(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b)$, the relation network outputs

$$
\boldsymbol{\pi}(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b)
=
[\pi_{+1},\pi_0,\pi_{-1}],
$$

where the three components denote that the first solution is superior, the two solutions belong to the same coarse quality group, and the second solution is superior, respectively. For an unevaluated candidate $\mathbf{x}$, four sets of ordered comparisons are constructed:

$$
(\mathcal{C}_1,\mathbf{x}),\quad
(\mathbf{x},\mathcal{C}_1),\quad
(\mathcal{C}_2,\mathbf{x}),\quad
(\mathbf{x},\mathcal{C}_2).
$$

Let $\mathbf{a}(\mathbf{x})$, $\mathbf{b}(\mathbf{x})$, $\mathbf{c}(\mathbf{x})$, and $\mathbf{d}(\mathbf{x})$ denote the corresponding group-wise mean probability vectors. The group-relative net evidence is

$$
r(\mathbf{x})
=2\left[
c_{-1}(\mathbf{x})+d_{+1}(\mathbf{x})
-a_{+1}(\mathbf{x})-b_{-1}(\mathbf{x})
\right],
\tag{10}
$$

where a larger value indicates stronger evidence that the candidate is comparable to the positive group and superior to the non-positive group. Since each probability vector sums to one,

$$
-4\le r(\mathbf{x})\le4.
\tag{11}
$$

In the exploration route, the group-wise probability vectors are averaged with the maximum class probability of each pair as its weight. This aggregation emphasizes sharper relation evidence without changing the semantics of the three relation classes.

### 4.2 Surrogate-assisted candidate-pool construction

The shared candidate pool is generated by an inner evolutionary search guided by the relation model. Genetic variation is first applied to the current population and reference solutions to obtain $\mathcal{Q}^{(0)}$. At inner iteration $\ell$, the relation network evaluates $\mathcal{Q}^{(\ell)}$, and the top

$$
n_{\mathrm{parent}}
=\min(|\mathcal{R}|,|\mathcal{Q}^{(\ell)}|)
$$

candidates are combined with the reference solutions to generate the next candidate set. All candidates generated during the inner search are accumulated and deduplicated:

$$
\mathcal{A}
=\operatorname{unique}
\left(\bigcup_{\ell=0}^{L}\mathcal{Q}^{(\ell)}\right),
\tag{12}
$$

where $L$ is determined by the candidate-generation limit $g_{\max}$. Both final selection routes operate on $\mathcal{A}$, so that they differ only in evaluation preference rather than candidate availability.

### 4.3 Ambiguity-and-distance-aware exploration route

Let $\mathcal{O}(\mathbf{x})$ contain all ordered comparisons between candidate $\mathbf{x}$ and the two quality groups. The mean predictive ambiguity is

$$
u(\mathbf{x})
=1-
\frac{1}{|\mathcal{O}(\mathbf{x})|}
\sum_{(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b)\in\mathcal{O}(\mathbf{x})}
\max_{y\in\{-1,0,+1\}}
\pi_y(\mathbf{x}_a,\mathbf{x}_b).
\tag{13}
$$

After min--max normalization within the candidate pool, the exploration score is

$$
a^{\mathrm{E}}(\mathbf{x})
=\widetilde r(\mathbf{x})
+\lambda_t\widetilde u(\mathbf{x}),
\tag{14}
$$

with

$$
\lambda_t
=\lambda_0
\left(1-\frac{FE}{B}\right)
\max\left(0,1-\frac{p_{\mathrm{err}}}{0.45}\right).
\tag{15}
$$

Thus, ambiguity sampling is emphasized earlier in the search and automatically reduced when the held-out relation-pair error increases. Candidates above the $q_{\mathrm{keep}}$ quantile form the preliminary set

$$
\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{E}}
=\left\{
\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{A}
\mid
a^{\mathrm{E}}(\mathbf{x})
\ge Q_{q_{\mathrm{keep}}}
\left(a^{\mathrm{E}}(\mathcal{A})\right)
\right\},
\tag{16}
$$

where $q_{\mathrm{keep}}=0.80$. If fewer than $n_{\min}$ candidates satisfy Eq. (16), the set is completed with the highest-scoring candidates. The first batch member maximizes $a^{\mathrm{E}}$. Each subsequent member maximizes

$$
A(\mathbf{x}\mid\mathcal{S})
=0.75\,\widehat a^{\mathrm{E}}(\mathbf{x})
+0.25\,\widehat d(\mathbf{x},\mathcal{S}),
\tag{17}
$$

where $d(\mathbf{x},\mathcal{S})=\min_{\mathbf{z}\in\mathcal{S}}\|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{z}\|_2$. This rule preserves relation quality as the primary criterion while distributing an evaluation batch across the decision space.

### 4.4 Front-shape-aware indicator route

The indicator route builds a ranking signal independently of the relation-network output. A generalized $L_p$ shape parameter is estimated from the current first nondominated front and used in the SDE fitness calculation `[PIEA]`. For normalized objective vectors, the shifted nearest-neighbor distance is

$$
\delta_i
=\min_{j\ne i}
\left\|
\bar{\mathbf{f}}_i-
\max(\bar{\mathbf{f}}_i,\bar{\mathbf{f}}_j)
\right\|_2.
\tag{18}
$$

When $\delta_i$ approaches zero, an $L_p$ distance to the current ideal point supplies additional discrimination. The resulting fitness values train an RBF-SVR model

$$
\widehat\phi(\mathbf{x})
=\operatorname{SVR}(\mathbf{x};\mathcal{P},\boldsymbol{\phi}).
\tag{19}
$$

The relation score first retains the top $30\%$ of the shared pool, with at least 20 candidates. The SVR then reranks this reduced set, and candidates not lower than the $70\%$ quantile of $\widehat\phi$ are retained. Relation evidence therefore excludes evidently low-priority regions before the SDE surrogate expresses front-shape and local-density preferences.

### 4.5 Stochastic route allocation and bounded expensive-evaluation batches

Let $m_t\in\{\mathrm{E},\mathrm{I}\}$ denote the route used at outer iteration $t$. A run-specific random stream produces $\xi_t\sim U[0,1)$, and

$$
m_t=
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{I}, & \text{if the indicator model is available and }\xi_t<p_{\mathrm{mix}},\\
\mathrm{E}, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\tag{20}
$$

We set $p_{\mathrm{mix}}=0.5$, giving the two candidate preferences equal prior exposure. If the indicator model is unavailable, the exploration route is used directly. The number of expensive evaluations is bounded by

$$
n_t
=\min\left[
n_{\max},
\max\left(n_{\min},|\mathcal{H}^{m_t}|\right)
\right],
\tag{21}
$$

and truncated further by the remaining evaluation budget. We use $n_{\min}=4$ and $n_{\max}=6$. Consequently, surrogate scores determine priority within a batch but no longer determine the evaluation expenditure of an outer iteration. If the selected set is empty, genetic variation on the population and reference solutions supplies at most $n_{\min}$ candidates.

Candidate-level experiments directly confirm that this mechanism changes the evaluated batch. Across four 20-objective problems with ten independent runs each, the dual-route selector was compared with direct relation-score top-6 selection from the same accumulated candidate pool. It increased the late-stage survival rate from $0.706$ to $0.770$ (paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test, $p=0.0085$) and the late-stage gain ratio relative to a greedy oracle from $0.0861$ to $0.1048$ ($p=0.0250$). The two measures have distinct responsibilities: survival records environmental acceptance, whereas the gain ratio measures set-level utility relative to the attainable gain from the same candidate pool.

### 4.6 Computational complexity

For a population of size $N$, a deduplicated pool of size $N_c$, decision dimension $D$, and objective dimension $M$, bidirectional relation scoring constructs $2N_cN$ network inputs and requires $O(N_cND)$ data construction and storage. SDE fitness calculation requires $O(N^2M)$ operations. The indicator route additionally fits one SVR on the $N$ evaluated solutions and predicts the relation-prefiltered candidates. The quality--distance batch construction costs $O(n_{\max}N_cD)$. All these operations occur before expensive evaluation and do not change the prescribed evaluation budget.
